Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

MacMixer is a macOS app that adjusts the volume of apps and browser tabs on your Mac. It is built to collect as little as possible: there are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no ads.

Audio

To change an app's volume, MacMixer routes that app's sound through its own volume control on your Mac. This requires the macOS Audio Recording permission. The audio is processed in real time on your Mac and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Browser extension

The optional extension sends the title, address and icon address of tabs that are playing sound to the MacMixer app, so it can show them in the mixer. This information only travels between your browser and the app on your own computer (through a local connection on 127.0.0.1) and is not sent to us or anyone else.

To show a tab's icon, the app may download that site's icon (favicon) directly from the website, as your browser does.

License checks

When you activate a license, MacMixer sends your license key and your Mac's name to Dodo Payments, our payment and licensing provider, so the activation can be listed under your license. Once a day, while you're online, the app sends the license key and activation ID to confirm the license is still valid. The provider receives your IP address as part of any internet request.

Updates

MacMixer checks for updates by downloading a public update list from this website, hosted on GitHub Pages. The request doesn't include any information about you or your Mac beyond what any web request includes (such as your IP address). You can turn automatic checks off in MacMixer › Settings.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Dodo Payments, which acts as Merchant of Record and collects the information needed to process your payment, deliver your license key and comply with tax rules. We receive your name, email address and order details so we can provide support. We don't receive or store your card details. See the Dodo Payments privacy policy.

Data on your Mac

Your volume settings are stored in the app's preferences on your Mac. Your license key and the trial start date are stored in your macOS Keychain. Deleting the app's preferences and Keychain items removes them.
