Refund Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

We want you to be happy with MacMixer. That's why there's a free 7-day trial with every feature, so you can make sure it works with your Mac, apps and audio devices before buying.

14-day money-back guarantee

If MacMixer doesn't work for you, ask for a full refund within 14 days of purchase. Email macmixer.app@gmail.com with the email address used for the purchase, or reply to your receipt. No long explanations needed, though we'd love to know what went wrong so we can fix it.

How refunds are processed

Refunds are issued by Dodo Payments, our Merchant of Record, to the original payment method. Depending on your bank, it may take 5 to 10 business days to appear. When a purchase is refunded, its license key is deactivated.

After 14 days

After 14 days, refunds are at our discretion. If something is broken, get in touch anyway and we'll do our best to help.
