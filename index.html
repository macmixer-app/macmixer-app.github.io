What it does

Volume per app

A slider and a mute button for each app that is playing sound: music, calls, games, videos.

Volume per browser tab

With the free browser extension, each tab in Chrome, Arc or Safari gets its own slider. Turn down a noisy tab without touching your call.

Remembers your levels

Each app keeps the volume you chose the next time it plays. MacMixer opens with your Mac and stays in the menu bar.

Stays out of the way

Apps at 100% are never touched. The browser volume works as a ceiling for its tabs, and system sounds are tucked away in their own section.

Private by design

Your audio never leaves your Mac. MacMixer adjusts the sound locally, and the browser extension only talks to the app on your own computer. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads. The app only goes online to check your license and look for updates.

FAQ

How does the free trial work?

Download MacMixer and use every feature for 7 days, no payment details needed. When the trial ends, the app stops adjusting volumes and all sound goes back to normal until you activate a license.

How do I activate my license?

After you buy, your license key arrives by email. Open MacMixer › Settings › License, paste the key and click Activate. You can deactivate it on one Mac to move it to another.

Which permissions does MacMixer need?

macOS asks for Audio Recording permission the first time you change a volume. MacMixer needs it to route each app's sound through its own volume control. Nothing is recorded or stored.

Do I need the browser extension?

No. Without it, MacMixer controls each browser as a whole. With it, every tab gets its own slider. Install it from MacMixer › Settings.

Does it work offline?

Yes. MacMixer checks your license once a day when you're online, and keeps working for up to 30 days without a connection.
